## 🚀 About

**LimpezaMac** is a native macOS application designed to help you keep your Mac clean, fast, and organized. Built with performance and simplicity in mind, it gives you full visibility and control over your system's storage, installed apps, background performance, and startup items.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🧹 1. Cleaning
* **Cache & Log Scanner:** Quickly find and safely remove temporary system caches and log files that clutter your disk.
* **Xcode Junk:** Reclaim massive amounts of storage by wiping out leftover Xcode derived data, simulators, and caches.
* **Trash Management:** Easily inspect and empty your system trash in one click.

### 📦 2. Applications
* **Installed Apps Overview:** View a comprehensive list of all applications installed on your Mac.
* **Path Inspector:** See exact file paths for every installed app to locate files effortlessly.
* **Smart Uninstaller:** Click on any app to completely remove it and its associated files from your system.

### ⚡ 3. Optimization
* **Startup Items Control:** Inspect applications and processes that launch automatically when you turn on your Mac.
* **Performance Boost:** Enable or disable startup items to drastically reduce boot times and free up background memory.

### 🗄️ 4. Space & Performance
* **Storage Analysis:** Deep-scan your hard drive to identify heavy files, folders, and directories consuming significant space.
* **Resource Insights:** Monitor what is running on your system to keep your Mac performing at its best.
